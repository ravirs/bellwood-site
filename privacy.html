Bellwood Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026 · Applies to Bellwood for iPhone, version 1.0 and later

Bellwood is a meditation timer. It is designed to collect nothing about you.

What Bellwood collects

Nothing. Bellwood has no account or sign-in, and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking software. It does not send any information about you or your practice to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

When you save a sitting, Bellwood stores when it started and ended, how long you sat and whether you finished. It also stores your settings, such as your preferred length, bells and reminder time. This information is kept only on your iPhone. It is excluded from iCloud and computer backups, so it is not copied anywhere else. If you delete Bellwood or lose your iPhone, this information is lost.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. If you allow them, Bellwood schedules them on your iPhone to ring the end bell while your screen is locked and, if you turn it on, to send one daily reminder at the time you choose. No server is involved.

Links to websites

The Help screen contains a link to an external website about meditation courses. It opens in Safari only if you tap it. That website has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

Delete a single sitting from History, or everything (history, settings and scheduled notifications) from Settings › Delete all data. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

Children

Bellwood collects no information from anyone, including children.
