Bellwood Support

Bellwood is a free meditation timer for iPhone. Here are answers to common questions.

The end bell didn't ring while my screen was locked

With the screen locked, the end bell is delivered as a notification. Check that notifications are allowed for Bellwood (Settings › Notifications › Bellwood). Focus modes, the Ring/Silent switch and your ringer volume can also silence it. Your sitting is always timed correctly, and the result is shown when you return to Bellwood.

My sitting says "Interrupted"

If your iPhone restarts, or its clock is changed while Bellwood is closed, Bellwood can't measure the time that passed in between. It shows only the time it recorded before then, and you can save or discard the sitting.

Is my history backed up?

No. For privacy, your history stays only on your iPhone and is not included in iCloud or computer backups. Deleting Bellwood removes it.

How do I delete my data?

Swipe a sitting in History to delete it, or use Settings › Delete all data to remove everything.
